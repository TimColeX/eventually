Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

This Privacy Policy explains how TIM COLE (“Eventually”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Eventually web application at eventually-app.com (the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

Eventually is a web application that shows live events around the world on an interactive globe, with an AI-generated audio “host” that describes what’s happening near you. The data controller responsible for your information is TIM COLE, contactable at info@eventually-app.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — when you create an account: your name, sign-in email address, and (optionally) a contact email and phone number.
  • Preferences — your saved events, interests, and communication preferences.
  • Events you submit — if you publish an event, the details you enter.
  • Live updates you post — if you post an update to an event you organize, the text of that update and the fact that your account posted it. These are shown publicly to anyone viewing the event while it is taking place.
  • Event registrations — if you register for an event that collects registrations through Eventually, we record that you registered and when. Your name and the email address on your account are shown to that event’s organizer so they can admit you. See Section 5.

Information collected automatically

  • Location — with your permission, your approximate GPS location, or a city you type or select, used to show nearby events and tailor the AI host. You can use the Service without granting GPS access by searching for a city instead.
  • Usage & device data — basic technical data such as browser type, device type, general region, pages viewed, and interactions, used to operate and improve the Service.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — we record a small set of anonymous actions (for example: the app was opened, the AI host was started, a city was searched, an event was opened) together with a random identifier stored in your browser and, where relevant, a city name. This is first-party: it stays on our own servers and is not shared with any analytics provider. It contains no name, email, account id or IP address, and cannot be used to identify you. We use it only to understand which features are used, and we delete it after 180 days.
  • Cookies & local storage — see Section 7.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the Service — show local events, generate audio briefings, and remember your saved events and settings.
  • To register you for events you choose to register for, and to email you a confirmation.
  • To email you reminders about events you saved or registered for. These are service messages about your own activity and are on by default; you can switch them off at any time in Profile → Account details, or with the unsubscribe link in any of them.
  • To send a weekly “what’s on near you” email — only if you switch it on yourself. It is never on by default, it uses the location saved on your profile to choose events, and every one carries a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To create and manage your account and any subscription.
  • To communicate with you about the Service, and (only with your consent) marketing.
  • To play sponsor messages and show featured events (see Section 6).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the UK/EU GDPR applies, we process your data on the bases of: performance of a contract (to provide the Service), consent (for location access and marketing — which you may withdraw at any time), legitimate interests (to secure and improve the Service), and legal obligation where required.

Sharing with event organizers

Some events let you register through Eventually. If you choose to register for one, we give that event’s organizer your name, the email address on your account, and the date you registered, so they can plan for you and check you in. This happens only for events you personally register for, and only that organizer (and our administrators) can see it — it is never shown to other attendees or to the public, which sees only a count.

You control this: nothing is shared until you tap Register, and you can cancel your registration at any time from the event. Organizers are permitted to use these details for that event only and are not permitted to add you to marketing lists.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

6. Sponsorship

The free Service is supported by sponsorship, in two forms (described in Section 9 of our Terms of Service):

  • Sponsor messages read by the AI audio host. Which message plays depends on the city whose briefing you are listening to, not on who you are — they are not personalised or targeted at you.
  • Featured events, marked “★ Featured”, which get more prominence on the globe and in lists. They are the same for everyone.

Sponsors receive none of your personal data. We may tell a sponsor how many times their message was played, as a total, but never who heard it.

We do not use third-party advertising networks or advertising cookies, and no advertiser tracks you on the Service. If that ever changes, we will update this Policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before any such cookies are used.

Subscribers to a paid ad-free tier (where offered) do not hear sponsor messages.

6a. Emails we send, and how to stop them

We send two kinds of email you can control, both to your sign-in address:

  • Event reminders — before an event you saved or registered for. These are on by default because they are about something you did; switch them off in Profile → Account details or via the unsubscribe link.
  • Weekly “what’s on near you” — off unless you turn it on. One email a week.

Every one of these carries a one-click unsubscribe that works without signing in. We also send service emails you cannot opt out of while you hold an account — sign-in links, registration confirmations, and messages about an event you published — because they are the Service doing what you asked it to.

We do not sell or share your email address with advertisers. Event organisers see it only when you register for their event (see Section 5).

7. Cookies & local storage

The Service uses cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and saved events, and enable the offline/installable app (service worker). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect functionality.

8. Data retention

Live updates are deleted automatically. They are visible only during a limited window around the event and are removed shortly after it ends. They are not archived.

Registrations are kept while the event is upcoming and for a short period afterwards, so organizers can reconcile who attended. Cancelling a registration removes it from the organizer’s list immediately; a copy they had already downloaded is then held by them rather than by us.

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Cached audio and event data are retained only as needed to operate the Service. You may delete your account at any time (see Section 9), after which we delete or anonymize your personal data except where retention is required by law.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@eventually-app.com. You can also edit your details or delete your account from within the app’s Profile. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

10. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with an updated “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

14. Contact us

Questions about this Policy or your data? Contact TIM COLE at info@eventually-app.com.
