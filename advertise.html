Advertise on Eventually

Reach people at the moment they are deciding what to do tonight — in the city you care about, in a voice they are already listening to.

Who you are talking to

People open Eventually to find something to do. They spin a globe, tap the place they are in (or a place they are going), and the AI host tells them what’s on there — like a local radio show tuned to right now. That is a short, high-intent moment, and it happens city by city.

Eventually lists events worldwide, from ticketed concerts and matches to free community events published directly by local organisers. Audience numbers vary a lot by city, so we don’t quote a global figure: tell us the city you want and we’ll send you the current numbers for it, honestly, including when they are small.

Promoting your own event instead?

If you are running the event, you don’t need any of the above. Publish it on Eventually — it appears on the globe like every other event — and you can ask us to feature it, which gives it a ★ badge, a taller marker and first place in its city’s list. Featuring is free while we’re in beta. Publish an event.

How we report

  • Host reads: how many times your message aired, and where.
  • Placements, when they launch: views, clicks and click-through rate, with a city breakdown. We count a view only when the placement was actually on screen — not merely loaded — so the numbers are smaller and mean something.
  • We’ll tell you what the numbers are before you book, not only after.

How we treat the people who see it

This is the part we would rather compete on.

  • No advertising networks, no advertising cookies, no tracking. Nobody is followed around the internet because they used Eventually.
  • Sponsorship is targeted by place, not by person. Your message appears in the city you chose, to whoever is looking at that city. We don’t build profiles, and we don’t target anyone by who they are or what they have done.
  • Sponsors receive no personal data — only counts.
  • Everything is identified. Spoken sponsorships are introduced as sponsor messages; placements are marked Sponsored.
  • No pop-ups, interstitials, auto-playing video, or anything that covers what someone came to read.
  • Sponsorship never changes the events we show or the order they appear in, except where an organiser’s event is marked ★ Featured, which is always visible as such.
  • Subscribers to our ad-free tier, where offered, don’t hear sponsor messages.

We review what we run, and we turn down anything we wouldn’t want a listener to hear — including anything misleading, or aimed at children.
