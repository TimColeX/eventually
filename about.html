About Eventually

Eventually is a live directory of more than 50,000 upcoming events around the world — shown on an interactive 3D globe, with two AI radio hosts who tell you what’s on near you.

What we’re building

Most event sites make you know what you’re looking for before you start. Eventually flips that around. Open the globe and you can literally see where the world is alive tonight: concerts, festivals, matches, talks, markets and more, glowing as markers wherever they’re on. Spin to a city, tap a glowing spot, and everything happening there appears at once — so discovery feels like exploring, not searching.

How it works

  • Explore the globe. Brighter, bigger markers mean busier hotspots; the colour tells you the main type of event at that spot.
  • Listen to the hosts. Press play and two AI radio hosts, David and Sarah, talk you through what’s worth knowing — and they follow you, so searching another city retunes them to it. Like a local station for wherever you are looking.
  • Save what catches your eye. Star any event to keep it in your personal calendar, with live “starts in” countdowns and reminders.
  • Times in the venue’s clock. A 7pm gig in Lagos says 7pm wherever you are reading it, with the zone named when it differs from yours — so you never work out the maths and get it wrong.
  • Get tickets from the source. When you want to go, Eventually sends you straight to the official seller, so you always book with the provider running the event.

Where the events come from

We aggregate live event data from trusted providers — Ticketmaster and its family of ticketing brands, and Showpass — alongside published calendar feeds from venues, theatres, libraries and universities, and combine it all into one map of the world. Repeat sittings of the same show are grouped into a single entry so a standing attraction does not crowd out everything else. Organisers can add their own events too. Every listing links out to the official seller or organiser.

You can also browse by place rather than by globe: every city we cover has its own page of what is coming up.

Free to use

Browsing, saving, and listening to the AI host are free. We keep the lights on with light, unobtrusive advertising. An optional membership, Eventually Plus, is planned for people who want advanced AI briefings with personal event picks, an ad-free experience, and travel-aware features.
