About Eventually

Eventually is a live directory of events around the world — shown on an interactive 3D globe, with an AI audio host that tells you what’s happening near you.

What we’re building

Most event sites make you know what you’re looking for before you start. Eventually flips that around. Open the globe and you can literally see where the world is alive tonight: concerts, festivals, matches, talks, markets and more, glowing as markers wherever they’re on. Spin to a city, tap a glowing spot, and everything happening there appears at once — so discovery feels like exploring, not searching.

How it works

  • Explore the globe. Brighter, bigger markers mean busier hotspots; the colour tells you the main type of event at that spot.
  • Listen to your AI host. Press play and a friendly audio host briefs you on what’s worth knowing near you — like a local radio show tuned to right now.
  • Save what catches your eye. Star any event to keep it in your personal calendar, with live “starts in” countdowns and reminders.
  • Get tickets from the source. When you want to go, Eventually sends you straight to the official seller. We’re a discovery platform — we don’t sell tickets ourselves.

Where the events come from

We aggregate live event data from trusted providers (such as Ticketmaster and PredictHQ) and combine it into one map of the world. Anyone can also add their own event to the globe in a few taps.

Free to use

Browsing, saving, and listening to the AI host are free. We keep the lights on with light, unobtrusive advertising. An optional membership, Eventually Plus, is planned for people who want longer personalized briefings, an ad-free experience, and travel-aware features.

Who’s behind it
